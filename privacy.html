Privacy Policy — KAT-AI Finance Backup Last updated: October 26, 2026 KAT-AI Finance Backup is a private, personal-use application operated by its owner to back up and restore financial research files using Google Drive. Google Drive access The application requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to create, access, and manage files used for its backup and recovery operations. Data storage and encryption Backup file contents and filenames are encrypted locally using rclone crypt before being uploaded to Google Drive. Encryption credentials are maintained separately from the uploaded backup files. Data sharing The application is not offered to the public. The operator does not sell personal data or intentionally share Google Drive data with advertisers or other third parties. Encrypted backup files are stored using Google Drive. Data retention and deletion Backup files are retained for recovery purposes and can be deleted by the owner. The owner can revoke the application’s Google Drive access through Google Account settings. Contact For questions about this application or its privacy practices, contact the developer using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
